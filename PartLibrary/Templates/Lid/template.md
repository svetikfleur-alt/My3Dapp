# Lid / Cover

- **ID:** `lid`
- **Category:** Enclosures
- **Tags:** lid, cover, cap, box

## Description

Printable cover plate with a shallow locating lip for small boxes, trays, and project enclosures.

## Parameters

| Key | Default | Unit | Notes |
|---|---:|---|---|
| width | 80 | mm | Overall width |
| depth | 60 | mm | Overall depth |
| thickness | 3 | mm | Top thickness |
| lipHeight | 4 | mm | Lip depth |
| tolerance | 0.4 | mm | Fit clearance |

## Geometry notes

The current lid generator builds a printable top plate and a simple locating lip. It is suitable for early enclosure workflows and printable fit experiments.
