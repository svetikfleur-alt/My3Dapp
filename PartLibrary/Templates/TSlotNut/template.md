# T-Slot Nut

- **ID:** `t-slot-nut`
- **Category:** Fasteners & hardware
- **Tags:** t-nut, t-slot, extrusion, 2020, hardware

## Description

Printable T-slot nut starter for aluminum extrusion framing and accessories.

## Parameters

| Key | Default | Unit | Notes |
|---|---:|---|---|
| slotWidth | 6 | mm | 2020 default |
| nutLength | 20 | mm | Nut body length |
| nutHeight | 3 | mm | Nut body thickness |
| holeDiameter | 3.2 | mm | Center hole diameter |
| flangeWidth | 10 | mm | T-flange outer width |

## Geometry notes

Current MVP geometry favors a stable printable block and center-hole workflow over detailed thread modeling.
